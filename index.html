My Role & Ownership

Ownership

During my time at BMW, my scope expanded quickly — I moved from junior, to mid-level, to senior designer within two years, reflecting the trust I built with design leadership and engineering. Each step brought more ownership across UI, motion, and platform work, and taught me to communicate design rationale clearly and partner closely with engineering. I stayed embedded with research, UX, and engineering for the full life of every feature I owned — that constant partnership is how I made sure what shipped was a quality product.

Visual Design, Grounded in UX

Rooting visual design in UX value

Every styling choice traces to a UX law or research insight — not personal taste.

01

Hick's Law

Surfaced only what a driver checks first — deferred the rest.

02

Law of Proximity

Grouped related data so the layout itself carries meaning.

03

Aesthetic-Usability Effect

Premium craft makes the product read as more trustworthy & usable — where brand becomes UX value.

04

Fitts's Law

Sized primary targets for fast, confident, glanceable taps.

05

Jakob's Law

Kept familiar platform patterns so styling never cost clarity.

06

Von Restorff Effect

Used contrast & color to make the key metric impossible to miss.
